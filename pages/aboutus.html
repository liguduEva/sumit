About Sweet Crumbs Pops Bakery

Bringing joy, warmth, and delicious artisanal baked treats to the Pretoria community since our founding.

Our Bakery Story

Sweet Crumbs Pops Bakery started as a passion project focused on perfecting handcrafted cake pops and slow-fermented breads. Over the years, we have grown into a favorite local bakery in Pretoria, serving families, corporate events, and celebrations.

Mission & Vision

Mission: To make fresh, delicious, and high-quality baked products while giving every customer friendly and memorable service.

Vision: To grow and become one of the most trusted and cherished artisanal bakeries across Gauteng.
